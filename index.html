Save, back up & share

Manual selections are automatically saved on this device. AP-called Democratic wins are marked blue for everyone, without editing your saved picks. Other visitors get their own independent copy. Your selections don't affect anyone else, and no account or server stores your picks. To move progress to another browser, copy and restore a backup.
