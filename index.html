Reading the cards: “Called” means Poliwave explicitly lists a winner; it is not a certification. “Count leader” means ahead in the reported votes. “Market favorite” describes traders’ prices, which can disagree with the count and do not become a call—even at 100%. Sources, dates & coverage

The national bars cover all 100 Senate seats, all 435 House seats and all 50 governorships, including offices not on this year’s ballot. Race cards cover all 35 Senate elections, a 92-district House watchlist containing all 17 currently unresolved House races, and 13 key governor elections. Counts on the bars are national results, not a sum of the selected cards.

Results: Poliwave SenatePoliwave HousePoliwave governors. Every race card links to its individual result page. The Texas Senate and governor cards use the Texas Secretary of State’s vote totals, with a separate link to the call source. Candidate names and vote totals have been refreshed from those result pages.

“If current count leaders win” is a count-based scenario, not a forecast. In Alaska, Maine and Louisiana, first-choice or first-round leaders may change after ranked-choice rounds or a runoff.
