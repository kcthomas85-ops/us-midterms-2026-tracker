02 · Trade prices, not election calls

Every House district with a published market price.

The 16 market prices below are the last traded Kalshi prices reported by State of the Odds on November 8 at 08:59 UTC. A listed percentage describes the market's price for the named candidate, not an official vote share or my own prediction. Only candidates with reported prices appear; the opposing party is displayed as the complement when the contract is a two-party winner market.

An illustrative 99% market price is not 99% of votes counted. A market printing 100% is not legal certification. Small-market prices, stale last trades, disputed counts, transfers and recounts can cause prices to move abruptly. Never sum prices from different races to claim an exact final-seat forecast.

How to read this dashboard: Poliwave provides a dated 435-seat call snapshot (not necessarily AP's). State of the Odds reproduces dated Kalshi last trades; a 99% price is an exchange indication, not an election call. State election authorities supply procedural deadlines. “Last Democrat” uses the last election or term verified by the linked source, with redistricting caveats. Candidate firsts use sources such as Roll Call and Rutgers CAWP. No invented race probabilities, refresh schedules or historical records. Data checked November 8, 2026.
