05 / A label with more than one definition

“Tsunami” is a description.
Wave thresholds are a test.

There is no official legal category called a blue tsunami. Ballotpedia uses an explicit historical method based on the largest fifth of election swings against the president’s party. Applying a test is more informative than choosing the biggest adjective.

06 / Every number has a trail

Check the snapshots. Reproduce the comparisons.

Forecasts and poll averages are frozen at the dates shown. Results are unofficial snapshots. Numbers on this page do not update automatically when a source changes.

Methods and limits

Seat totals, flips, popular-vote margins and chamber control are kept separate. We use the provider’s calls without certifying them ourselves, and preserve the House tracker’s existing result provider for consistency. A probability-based estimate or an uncalled lead is not substituted for a media call.

Polling gaps use the same raw-vote snapshot for all three comparisons, include other-party votes in the denominator and round only at the end. They are provisional national-margin comparisons, not a district-level error study. Selected historical comparisons do not establish an all-time record.

No claim is made that switching Trump voters alone caused the outcome. A vote swing can combine turnout changes, candidate differences, switching and other factors; aggregate results cannot separate those causes.
